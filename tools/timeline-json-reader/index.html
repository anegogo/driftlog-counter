How to get your Timeline.json

  1. On your phone, open Google Maps → your profile picture → Your Timeline.
  2. Tap ⋮ → Location and privacy settings → Export Timeline data.
  3. Save or share the exported file, then drop it in above. On desktop, Google Takeout’s old Records.json also works.

Want it to keep filling in by itself?

Driftlog is the iPhone app that does this automatically: it records the countries and cities you pass through in the background — no check-ins, no account, no server, no subscription. It can also rebuild past trips from your photo library, the same way this page does.
